Four ways to play

  • SwipeLeft to skip, right to accept.
  • Blind voteTwo of you pick in secret, then find out if you matched.
  • Table rouletteThe wheel spins and picks who is up. Multi-touch, so the whole table can tap.
  • Flip to defuseA speed round with a timer. Answer, then turn the phone over.
