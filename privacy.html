Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Spill is a party game for iPhone. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

Spill does not ask for your name, your email address, your location, your contacts or your photos. It does not contain advertising. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Everything you need to play is on your device.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps a small amount of information in its own private storage area on your iPhone. It never leaves the device.

WhatWhyWhere it lives

None of this is linked to an account, and none of it is sent to us.

Motion and haptics

The app reads the device's motion sensors to detect when you turn the phone over, which is how the "flip to defuse" round works. Motion data is processed on your device, in memory, and is never stored or transmitted.

The app also uses the device's haptic engine for touch feedback. That runs entirely on the device.

Purchases

The app offers an optional one-time unlock and an optional auto-renewing subscription. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store, and Apple's servers handle your payment details — the app never sees your card number.

The app talks to Apple's StoreKit framework directly. There is no third-party purchase SDK, no purchase-analytics service and no purchase middleware of any kind. When you buy something, Apple sends the transaction to the app so that the app can unlock the feature, and that is the only purchase data the app ever handles. It stays on your device.

If you subscribe, Apple's servers receive the transaction, the product identifier, the price and a transaction identifier that may be linked to an Apple ID. Apple handles that data under its own privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

To restore a purchase on a new device, the app asks Apple's servers for the transactions already associated with your Apple ID. That request goes to Apple only.

Network requests

The app is designed to work offline. When it is online, it contacts only:

  • Apple's servers, to fetch the price of the plans, to complete a purchase, and

to restore past purchases.

No other request is made. The app contains no third-party advertising and no analytics SDK of any kind.

Third-party SDKs

There are none. The app uses only Apple's own frameworks — StoreKit for purchases, CoreMotion for the flip round, CoreHaptics for feedback — plus SwiftUI.

Children

Spill is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal information, there is generally nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete. If you want to remove everything the app has stored on your device, delete the app — the operating system removes its private storage along with it. If you want to stop restoring purchases to a new device, you can do that from the App Store settings on your device.

International transfers

The app does not transfer your data to us or to any third party, so there is no cross-border transfer of your information to declare. Purchases are handled by Apple under its own privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of it and publish the new version here. Material changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
