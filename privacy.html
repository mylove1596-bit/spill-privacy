Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Spill is a party game for iPhone. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

Spill does not ask for your name, your email address, your location, your contacts or your photos. It does not contain advertising. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Everything you need to play is on your device.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps a small amount of information in its own private storage area on your iPhone. It never leaves the device unless a purchase takes place.

None of this is linked to an account, and none of it is sent to us.

Motion and haptics

The app reads the device's motion sensors to detect when you turn the phone over, which is how the "flip to defuse" round works. Motion data is processed on your device, in memory, and is never stored or transmitted.

The app also uses the device's haptic engine for touch feedback. That runs entirely on the device.

Purchases

The app offers an optional one-time unlock and optional auto-renewing subscriptions. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store, and Apple's servers handle your payment details — the app never sees your card number.

The store receipt and the resulting entitlement are passed to RevenueCat, our analytics and subscription provider, so that we can confirm your purchase, restore it on a new device and understand how the paywall is used. RevenueCat acts as a processor on our behalf. RevenueCat's own privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

If you subscribe, Apple's servers receive the transaction, the product identifier, the price and a transaction identifier that may be linked to an Apple ID. We receive the product identifier, the purchase date, the expiry date and an anonymous app user identifier.

Network requests

The app is designed to work offline. When it is online, it contacts only:

  • Apple's servers, to fetch the price of the plans and to complete a purchase.
  • RevenueCat's servers, to sync your entitlement and, if you opt in, to record

anonymous usage events.

No other request is made. The app contains no third-party advertising and no analytics SDK other than RevenueCat.

Children

Spill is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal information, there is generally nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete. If you want to remove everything the app has stored on your device, delete the app — the operating system removes its private storage along with it. If you want to stop syncing your entitlement to a new device, you can do that from the App Store settings on your device.

International transfers

RevenueCat may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where that happens, the transfer is covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of it and publish the new version here. Material changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
