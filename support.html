Support

Getting help

If something is not working, or you have a question about your purchase, we would like to hear from you.

Email: support@spill.app

Please include:

  • the model of your iPhone and the iOS version (Settings → General → About)
  • what you did, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead
  • a screenshot if the problem is visual

We answer support email within a few business days.

Frequently asked

How do I restore my purchase? Open the app, tap the Pro button on the home screen, then tap "Restore purchases" at the bottom of the paywall. Your Pro access returns immediately.

I bought Pro on another device. Use the same "Restore purchases" button. The purchase is tied to your Apple ID, not to your device.

How do I cancel a subscription? Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → choose the subscription → Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

Do I need an internet connection? No. Every prompt and every card is on your device. The app only needs a connection to confirm a purchase or to refresh your Pro access.

The app says a prompt is locked. Prompts in the highest intensity band are part of Pro. You can skip past them and carry on with the free deck, or unlock everything.

Does the app record anything? No. See the privacy policy for the details. Nothing about your session is uploaded.

Why does the app ask for motion access? To detect when you turn the phone over, which is how the "flip to defuse" round works. There is always a button that does the same thing.

Is this an alcohol game? No. Spill does not reference alcohol anywhere. It uses the word "sips" as a made-up, virtual token for a penalty. Nothing is consumed and nothing is purchased in the real world.

Reporting a problem

We are glad to hear about bugs. If you find one, please email support@spill.app with the steps to reproduce it. We will look into it.

If you believe you have found a security issue, please email support@spill.app with "security" in the subject line rather than opening a public issue.
